You ask an AI to build an app that works offline. Later, it suggests a change that requires internet.

I built Owner’s Vision to help catch that kind of drift.

It’s a set of instructions for your AI coding tool. Before new work starts, it tells the AI to check the plan against your saved requirements and what already works.

It also reminds you to record finished, verified work. New lines are added only with your explicit permission. Earlier lines stay unchanged.

The 27-second demo shows a simple example.

Free and open source under MIT:
https://github.com/AmmarAlBalkhi/owners-vision
