Before an AI changes your project, does its next step still fit your vision?

I built Owner’s Vision: a skill that tells AI coding agents to check new work against your vision and verified progress.

PASS: the plan fits your requirements and preserves verified progress. Already-authorized work can continue.

FAIL → PAUSE: a conflict, missing proof, or incomplete review stops implementation. The agent reports the blocker to you.

The skill requires a vision-integrity check and a fresh independent review before PASS. You remain in control of what gets authorized.

The 24-second video shows the decision flow.
