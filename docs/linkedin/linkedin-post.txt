As an AI agent keeps iterating, a project can gradually drift away from your original vision—even when each individual step seems reasonable.

I built Owner’s Vision to keep your requirements and verified progress at the centre of each new step.

It’s an open-source skill for AI coding agents. Before implementation, it requires the agent to confirm the saved vision is intact and review the proposed work. It uses a fresh subagent when supported, or the same agent in the current session otherwise.

✅ PASS: The plan fits your vision and preserves completed work. Work you’ve already authorized can continue.

🛑 PAUSE: Something conflicts or cannot be verified. The agent is instructed to pause and explain the blocker.

It may be especially useful for vibe coders moving quickly through iterations who want a consistent check on the project’s direction.

The picture shows the check, the accomplishment reminder, and the lines added only after the owner authorizes them.
